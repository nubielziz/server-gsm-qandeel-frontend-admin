# 026 One Money page replaces Finance and Refunds & disputes

**Context.** The sidebar's Money group had two pages: Finance (`/finance`: Ledger, Deposits, Trial balance, Store balances, Invoices and Reports, under a strip of five figures and a "Run reconciliation" button) and Refunds & disputes (`/refunds`: Refunds, Card disputes and Payments, under a strip of four figures). Staff who look after money had to switch between them, and each page had its own tab bar. The owner asked (2026-11-25) for one page called Money in the layout of the Accounts page (decision 023), which the team already knows, with the backend left as it is. The owner chose the grouping below, an Overview tab first (the Accounts layout has no row of figures, so without it the figures would be gone) and "+Issue invoice" as a full view, like "+Add Account".

**Decision.**
- **What the page is.** Resource `money`, path `/money`, heading and sidebar label "Money", the only entry of the Money group, icon `wallet`. Any one of `ledger:read`, `payments:read`, `wallet:read`, `invoices:read`, `reports:read`, `reports:finance`, `reports:growth`, `refunds:read` or `disputes:read` opens it; a role with none sees "Nothing here for your role". The sidebar count is the pending deposits (for a role that may use Deposits) plus the open refunds (for one that may use Refunds), with the late dot of the dispute deadlines within three days (Card disputes); a dot is never drawn without a number. `g f` opens Money; `g r` is retired.
- **Level 1, the tabs.** A strip of **Overview | Money in | Money back | Books | Reports**. Money in counts the deposits waiting for review; Money back the refunds to decide or retry plus the disputes that need evidence. A tab that holds no view the role may use is not drawn; a tab's link opens its first view the role may use.
- **Level 2, the views.** Text links under the tab, each a full view with its own address (`?view=<id>`; the Overview needs none):
  - Overview: the Finance figures (bank, merchant wallets, held in open orders, supplier prepaid, deposits waiting; each for the role that may read it, and the whole finance part left out for a role that reads none), the refund and dispute figures, "Run reconciliation" (shown with `ledger:read`, enabled with `ledger:reconcile`) and a "Waiting for you" list. No row of links.
  - Money in: **Deposits (n)** (`deposits`, `payments:read`) · **Shop payments** (`shop-payments`, `reports:finance`: the old Refunds page's Payments tab).
  - Money back: **Refunds (n)** (`refunds`, `refunds:read`) · **Card disputes (n)** (`disputes`, `disputes:read`).
  - Books: **Ledger** (`ledger`) · **Trial balance** (`trial-balance`), both `ledger:read` · **Store balances** (`store-balances`, `wallet:read`) · **Invoices** (`invoices`, `invoices:read`) · **+Issue invoice** (`issue-invoice`: `invoices:read`, `invoices:create` and `clients:read` for the merchant picker; the Invoices toolbar loses its button). The form is the old dialog's (same fields, checks and server messages) on a label-left page with a pinned "Issue invoice" button and the unsaved-changes guard; one press sends one request (the route takes no idempotency key), and a success opens the new invoice's drawer on Invoices.
  - Reports: **Profit** (`profit`) · **Wallet exposure** (`exposure`), both `reports:read` · **VAT** (`vat`, `reports:finance`) · **Sales funnel** (`funnel`, `reports:growth`). The report switch of the old Reports tab is gone; each report keeps its choices (`by`, `range`, `period`) in the address.
  A view the role may not use, named in the address, is replaced by the first view of the same tab it may use, then by its first view; an unknown view is its first view. The tables, filters, dialogs and drawers inside the views are the old tabs' own, with the same address keys (`status`, `method`, `page`, `sort`, `dir`, `payment`, `journal`, `invoice`, `order`, `account` and the others).
- **Links from other pages follow the view.** The Money page opens for many permissions, so a palette action, a dashboard button or an audit row that opens a Money view is offered only to a role that may use that view (`canUseView`), not merely open the page.
- **One address builder.** `src/features/money/lib/moneyViews.ts` holds the views, their permissions and `moneyPath(view, params)`; `financePath`, `financePaymentPath`, `financeJournalPath`, `financeInvoicePath` and `refundsPath` keep their names for the pages that link here and now write Money addresses.
- **Where the code lives.** The views stay in the finance and refunds features (their API functions, types and mocks do not move, so the backend's contract of the console does not change). Each feature has a `views.ts` that the Money page loads on demand (`loadFinanceViews`, `loadRefundViews` in their indexes): a static import would be a cycle, because the accounts and orders features import the finance index and the views import them back. The `money` feature holds the frame: the page, the two rows of links, the address, the Overview.
- **Old addresses redirect for good** (`legacyTarget`): `/finance` is `/money?view=ledger`; `?tab=payments|accounts|wallets|invoices` is `view=deposits|trial-balance|store-balances|invoices`; `?tab=reports&report=profit|exposure|vat|funnel` is that report's view (Profit without a `report`); `/refunds` is `/money?view=refunds` and `?tab=disputes|payments` is `view=disputes|shop-payments`. Every other key of the old address is carried. `/commissions` is `/money?view=ledger`; `/checkouts` is `/money?view=funnel` (with `range=7|90`).

**Consequences.**
- No backend change and no new route: the page calls exactly the routes the two pages called.
- Links into the two old pages from the dashboard, the palette, the audit log, orders, accounts and System health go through the builders above.
- The tests of the two pages moved to the new addresses; the frame has its own tests (views by role, fallbacks, counts, the redirects).
