# 020 Merchant verification by documents is retired

**Context.** The map gives merchants a verification step: staff review documents a merchant sends, and each merchant row carries a
verification word (`kycStatus`). The owner decided on 2026-11-23 that this step is cancelled: the owner creates every merchant
account, so nobody sends documents.

**Decision.** The console drops everything of it. The approval of an account (the pending banner, the Approve and Decline buttons,
the Pending approval tab) is a separate step and stays as it is (it was retired later, decision 021).
- The Verification tab of the merchants list and the Verification tab of a merchant's page are gone, with their table, side sheet,
  decision dialog, status badge, hooks, types, mock routes and mock rows. A merchant's API keys keep their types (`types/apiKeys.ts`)
  and their mock routes (`mocks/apiKeyRoutes.ts`), which used to share a file with the verification ones.
- The console stops calling the three routes the map lists for it (GET list, GET one, POST decide) and the `kyc-submissions` Refine
  resource is gone. `scripts/check-route-coverage.mjs` lists the three as excluded with this decision as the reason, so coverage stays
  at 100% and the route manifest still mirrors the map.
- The merchant row has no verification word: `Client` has no `kycStatus`, the mocks do not send it, the pending banner says only
  "The merchant cannot place orders until you approve the account."
- The two permissions of the area leave the console: the page opens with `clients:read` or `groups:read`, the "Customers and
  merchants" role area and the mock permission catalog no longer list them. A role that still holds one in the database is harmless to
  the console; the backend removes them from stored roles.
- The demo seed loses the verification word of its merchants (`scripts/retired-seed.mjs`, also applied by `seed:extract`).
- An old address keeps working: `?tab=verification` (with or without `kyc` or `kycStatus`) on the list and on a merchant's page
  opens the first tab; the other parameters of the address still apply. Tests cover both pages and the helpers.

**Compatibility.** The console works against a backend that still sends `kycStatus` and still has the routes (the field is ignored,
the routes are never called) and against one that has removed both. Release order: console first, backend last. The generated files
that mirror the map or the backend stay as they are until their sources change: `src/lib/api/schema.gen.ts` (row types from the
map's tables), `docs/route-manifest.*` and `docs/slices/` (the map's routes), the English description files of the map's rows.
`src/mocks/liveRoutes.gen.ts` loses the three routes now and is regenerated by `npm run mocks:live` after the backend merge.

**Consequences.** One list and one page less to keep; no merchant state besides active, pending and suspended. Rows M47 and M52 of
`docs/MISMATCHES.md` are updated and M278 records the change.
