# 023 One Accounts page replaces Merchants and Customers

**Context.** The console had two pages for the people who buy: Merchants (wholesale accounts with a wallet, a credit limit, API keys and fixed prices) and Customers (shop buyers with a store balance, consent records and privacy requests), each with its own list, its own record and its own family of routes. The backend's decision 064 makes them one thing: one account type, told apart by its rank. A rank is a client group; only a rank of the merchant kind makes a merchant, anything else is retail. A guest is an account row without a login. Every account has one live wallet, of the kind its rank asks for, and one staff action, the rank change, moves an account from one rank to another and moves the balance with it. The owner asked for the page of this model in the layout of the Product / Services page (decision 022), which their staff already know.

**Decision.**
- **What the page is.** Resource `accounts`, path `/accounts` (and `/accounts/:id`), heading and sidebar label "Accounts", in the Operations group after Orders, icon `users`. A role that holds `clients:read` or `groups:read` opens it; a role with `groups:read` alone lands on the Ranks view; a role with neither sees "Nothing here for your role". The Merchants and Customers pages leave the sidebar, the palette and the shortcuts: one Accounts palette source, `g m` opens Accounts, `g k` is retired.
- **Level 1, the population.** A strip of tabs, **All accounts | Retail | Merchants | Guests**, each with its count from `GET /admin/clients/counts` (`?chip=retail|merchants|guests`). On a record the strip gains a last raised tab named after the account. The strip scopes the All view only; on Ranks, Add Rank, Edit rank, Data requests and Add Account no tab is raised.
- **Level 2, the view.** Text links **All | Add Account (+) | Ranks | Add Rank (+)**, a divider, and **Data requests (n)** (`?view=all|add|ranks|add-rank|edit-rank|requests`; All needs no `view`). Every link is a full view with the grammar of decision 022: a Quick Search box, a list that ends in the footer pager, label-left forms that end in a pinned action bar; no side panel and no multi-step dialog. A view the role may not use is replaced by the first view it may use. Add Account needs `clients:read` and `clients:create`, Ranks `groups:read`, Add Rank `groups:read` and `groups:create`, Data requests `clients:read` and `customers:privacy`.
- **All.** The list of accounts: Account (name and email), Rank, Status, Wallet (only with `wallet:read`), Spent 30 d, Orders and Last order, with a row menu of the common actions; the "With selected" strip offers Suspend, Reactivate and Sign out everywhere, one request per account, and ends in a summary when the server refused some. No bulk action moves money, limits spending, mails people, blocks or anonymizes. The filters are the rank, the country, the status and the search over name, email, company and username; the default sort is account A to Z.
- **The record.** `/accounts/:id?tab=<part>`, drawn as the editor of decision 022: a left menu of parts and the part on the right; the record's name is the title of the menu and the page's heading stays "Accounts", so the record has no action header. Up to thirteen parts, in this order: Overview, Financial (`wallet`), Profile, Invoices, Payments, API, Security, Prices, Orders, Support, Statement, Privacy and risk (`privacy`) and Activity Log (`audit`). A part shows only to a role that may read what it holds and only for an account that has it: a merchant rank shows up to thirteen parts, a retail account with a login eleven, a guest seven; an anonymized account loses the parts that act on a live login (Financial, Profile, API, Security and Prices). An unknown, hidden or retired part opens the first visible part. Only Profile is a form with a pinned Save; every other part is read-only and acts through its own dialogs: Overview (Change rank…, Suspend…, Reactivate…, Make an account…), Financial (Adjust wallet…, Put on hold… or Lift hold…, Credit limit…, the official statement), Security (Password…, Sign out everywhere, Reset two-step sign-in…, Resend verification email, Block IP… for the shop audience), Privacy and risk (Export data…, Anonymize…, Block… or Unblock…), API (New key…, Limits…, Revoke…), Prices (Add a fixed price…).
- **One address builder.** `src/features/accounts/lib/paths.ts` writes every address of the page: `accountsListPath(state)` (keys in a fixed order, defaults left out), `accountPath(id, tab?, { order? })`, `ranksPath()` and `legacyAccountPath(type, oldId, tab?)`. Other pages link through it; none writes an address of its own. Deep links: `chip`, `status`, `rank`, `country`, `q`, `sort`, `dir`, `page`, `size`, `id` (the rank being edited), `request` (the status of the Data requests view); `tab` and `order` (the order drawer of the Orders part) on a record. The list page rewrites an address it reads differently (an old chip, an unknown value, a page past the end) and remembers the last list address of each tab for "Back to All" (`sessionStorage`, `gsm-accounts-list-address`).
- **Old addresses redirect for good**, through `legacyTarget` (`src/app/legacyRedirects.ts`), before the permission gate, replacing the entry and carrying the hash:
  - `/merchants` is `/accounts?chip=merchants` (`q`, `page`, `group` as `rank`, and `sort=name|status` with `dir` stay); `tab=suspended` is `chip=suspended` (the page reads it as `status=suspended`), `tab=groups` is `view=ranks`, any other tab is `chip=merchants`.
  - `/customers` is `/accounts?chip=retail` (`q`, `country` and `page` stay; the old sort has no counterpart); `tab=suspended|blocked|requests` is `chip=suspended|blocked|requests` (the page reads the first two as `status=` and the third as the Data requests view), any other tab is `chip=retail`.
  - `/merchants/:id` is `/accounts/:id` (a merchant keeps its number): `tab=orders|wallet|billing|pricing|api|profile|security|audit` becomes `orders|wallet|invoices|prices|api|profile|security|audit`; `order` stays.
  - `/customers/:id` is `/accounts/:id?from=customer`, because a customer number is not an account number (the customers were copied after the merchants): `tab=orders|account|wallet|support|consents|risk` becomes `orders|security|wallet|support|privacy|privacy`. The page, seeing `from`, shows only a loader, asks `GET /admin/legacy-accounts/:type/:id` and replaces the entry with the account's address, or says "This customer record moved or no longer exists"; it never reads or draws the account whose number equals the old customer number, and it says "That address is not an account number." for a value that is not a number. It needs `clients:read`. An id that is not a whole number from 1 up, and any address below the record, is not an old address.
- **Statuses.** Four words: active, suspended, blocked, anonymized. There is no `pending`: an account the old model called pending is read as suspended with the migration's fixed reason and comes back through "Reactivate…". A word the console does not know is printed as the server wrote it, with no banner and no button.
- **The rank change and the Owner rule.** Change rank… is a dialog of the Overview part that names the current rank ("Retail" or "<name>, merchant"), the choices ("<name>" for retail, "<name> (-8%)" for a merchant rank), the blockers the server lists in words (ten codes, `RANK_CHANGE_BLOCKED`) and the amount that moves with the account. It is offered to the Owner only; the Owner is a role granted exactly `*` (`isOwnerRole`). A merchant rank in Add Account, and New key…, are Owner only too. Every money write is a single-account dialog with one `Idempotency-Key` per opening (`useDialogKey`): the wallet adjustment of either wallet kind, hold and lift, the credit limit and the rank change; a retry of the same dialog sends the same key, the next opening a new one.
- **Ranks.** The Groups tab became the Ranks view: the list of ranks with their kind, discount, credit, deposit limit and number of accounts, Add Rank and Edit rank as views, Delete only in the "With selected" strip. A rank form checks its name, percent and deposit before it sends.
- **Anonymized and blocked accounts.** An anonymized account offers no action (no Export either); Privacy and risk says what was removed and the Orders part shows the IMEIs of its orders cut. Block sends one of four reasons exactly as the server takes them (no note). A blocked account's Overview says "Blocked for fraud:" and links to Privacy and risk.
- **Places that named a merchant or a customer** now name an account: Orders (the buyer is `account` with its rank; filters `accountId` and `rankId`; an old `clientId` in an address is read as `accountId`; the reopen of a completed order whose account now holds a retail rank says to refund it instead), Refunds, disputes and Risk (the buyer's key keeps its name and holds an account id), Finance (ledger owner `account`; store balances link to the Financial part; payments, invoices and the profit split by merchant read accounts; the ledger kind `wallet_transfer` reads "Rank change transfer"; wallet exposure adds `storeBalances` beside `totals`), Support (requester `account`; the Merchant tag and the Who control by rank kind; the Support part of a record is the ticket list of `accountId`), Notifications (the people picker has the tabs Staff and Accounts; segments by rank kind; recipient type `account`), Audit (entity and actor `account`, one filter "Account"; an old `customer` row goes through the legacy lookup, a `merchant` row keeps its number), the dashboard (one Top accounts panel, which needs `reports:read`; the negative-wallet button opens the merchant list sorted by balance), System health (the integrity check `wallets_follow_rank`), Admins and access (the role area "Accounts" with the titles Accounts, Ranks and Privacy; sessions of either kind with a Who filter, and ending an account's session needs `clients:security`) and Product / Services ("Merchant group" became "Rank"; the price-check lookup lists merchant accounts).
- **What the old pages did that this page does differently.** There is no "Activate account" (no pending status) and no Flagged tab or risk level (an old address opens All; `sort=riskLevel` opens the default sort). Security shows a count of active sessions, never a list of devices. The old "Show more" of the audit tab is the pinned pager (25 entries to start in Activity Log, 50 elsewhere; sizes 25, 50, 100, 200). The header actions moved into the parts that own them. The scope list of an API key is gone (a new key sends `scopes: ['dhru']`). Lifetime spend, refund and dispute figures are not shown, because the detail read carries only the 30-day figures and the order count.
- **The data layer.** `src/features/accounts` holds the types, one function per route with its `@route` tag, the hooks and keys (`ACCOUNT_WALLET_KEY = ['api', 'accounts', 'wallet']` replaces the merchant wallet key), the mock store and the test helpers. The error type keeps each field error's code (`fieldCodes`), so a refused rank change, "guest" and "login_exists" are told apart. The mock store is built from both demo lists: a merchant keeps its number, customer `cN` is account `100 + N`, and one nameless guest is account 131. Every `/admin/customers*` route answers 410 `ROUTE_RETIRED` in the mock layer, as the backend does. The four Refine resources `clients`, `client-groups`, `client-prices` and `data-requests` come from this feature only.
- **Generated files.** `src/mocks/liveRoutes.gen.ts` is made again from the backend's contract (`npm run mocks:live`), so the partial mock mode sends the new routes to the real API. `docs/ROUTE_COVERAGE.md` is made again by `npm run routes:coverage`.

**Consequences.**
- The two old features and their 497 test cases are gone; each case has a home in the Accounts suites. The page's tests run against the mock API and cover the list address, the strip, the parts by rank and by guest, each dialog and each refusal.
- The wire the console reads is in MISMATCHES M328 to M367. The ones that ask something of the backend: `requester.rankKind` and the `rankKind` filter on tickets, the `byType` keys `staff`, `merchant` and `retail` on a campaign's audience preview, `storeBalances` beside `totals` on the wallet exposure report, and the codes of `RANK_CHANGE_BLOCKED`.
- The console is released together with the backend's expand release and the shop's change (backend decision 064, shop decision 002): the old customer routes answer 410 from then on, and an older console would show empty pages.
- Left for a later version: lifetime figures on Overview (the detail read would have to carry them; the page leaves the rows out and a test says they are absent).
