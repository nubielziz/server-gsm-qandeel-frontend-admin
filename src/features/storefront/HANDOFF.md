# Storefront: handoff

Page `/storefront` ("Shop content", in the System group of the sidebar), 22 of 22 routes of `docs/slices/storefront.json`. Demo: `50-p-storefront.js` (Live preview excluded) with the helpers in
`12-state-b2c.js` and `13-b2c-ui.js`. Tabs, in the address as `?tab=`: Landing pages (the default, always written as `tab=landing`), Content and legal, Help centre, TAC catalogue (new), Search (new, feature 29).

> **2026-11-23, Shop content and the Product / Services page:** the page is "Shop content" and sits in the System group, after Settings; the Catalog group holds Product / Services and Suppliers only. Its former tabs Services on sale, Publish and Retail pricing are parts of the Product / Services page (`/products`, services feature, decision 022; the Catalog page of decision 019 was removed): the publish runs are its Shop Publish view (`/products?view=publish[&run=<id>]`), the retail pricing its Price Rules view on the tab Shop price rules (`/products?view=rules&tab=shop[&show=all]`) and the services on the shop its All view (`/products?view=all`). Their components stay in this feature and are exported through its index; `storefrontPublishPath`, `storefrontPricingPath` and `storefrontOnShopPath` return those addresses (built by the services feature's `productsPath`). The old addresses (`/catalog…`, `?tab=listings`, `?tab=publish`, `?tab=pricing`, and `/storefront` with `q`, `page`, `sort` or `dir` and no tab) are redirected by `src/app/legacyRedirects.ts`. The sections below describe the page as it was built; where they name those three tabs, read the Product / Services addresses above. The old Services on sale table (`ListingsTab`, its table, counts, empty state, create, edit and price dialogs, its test host and tests) was deleted when the Product / Services views were joined; the edit view's Retail Purchase part (`RetailPurchasePart`, `components/retailPurchase/`, exported from the index with plain props) shows and edits a service's shop page and shop price, on `ListingFormBody` (its `layout="row"`) and `shop/priceForm.ts`.

> **2026-11-16, English only (decision 039):** the console no longer has a language. The Language select is gone from the help article, content page, new legal version and add listing dialogs; the Language column is gone from the listings and landing page tables; `locale` is no longer sent or read on the listing, content page and help article routes (filters, `POST` and `PATCH` bodies, rows); the category has no Arabic name; the Search tab's picker sends no `locale`. A legal document is the one exception: its row keeps `locale` read-only, and a retired non-English version shows as `(retired TR text)` in "Earlier versions kept for the record". The rows STOREFRONT-7 and STOREFRONT-10 and the route notes below describe the earlier two-language design.

> **2026-11-17, Add listing searches the services:** the Service field of the Add listing dialog is a search box (a combobox), not a select. It asks `GET /admin/services?q=<text>&type=imei&status=active&_sort=name&_order=asc&_start=0&_end=20` 250 ms after the last keystroke (no `q` lists the first 20 by name), shows each match with its name and code, and says how many more match on the server. The select it replaces loaded the first 200 names only, which hid most services after a supplier's Quick add. No route, body or permission changed (`catalog:read` as before); the server still refuses a service that already has a listing, under the field.

## Assumed contracts

All marked "Assumed contract" in the JSDoc of `api/*.ts` and `types/*.ts`.

- **Listings** (`/admin/service-listings`): real contract of the backend's feature 22 (`listings.contracts.ts`), no longer assumed. A list row is the generated row plus `service { id, name, code, basePrice, cost }`, `retailPrice` (or `null`), `floorPrice`, `marginPct`, `orders30d`, `revenue30d`, `locked`, `lockedReason`, and since feature 22 `summary`, `etaText`, `brand`, `carrier`, `country`, `sort`, `seoTitle`, `seoDescription`, `listedAt` (or `null`), `imageUrl` (root-relative `/store/v1/media/<name>`, or `null`), `updatedAt`. Show, `POST` and `PATCH` answer the row plus `descriptionMd` and `requirementsMd` (the list leaves them out). Filters `q`, `isListed`, `locked`, `isFeatured`; sorts `publicTitle`, `isListed`, `slug`, `id`, `listedAt`, `sort`. `POST` body `{ serviceId, locale, publicTitle, slug?, ...content }`. `PATCH` body any of `{ isListed, retailPrice, publicTitle, slug, summary, descriptionMd, requirementsMd, etaText, seoTitle, seoDescription, brand, carrier, country, isFeatured, sort }`, at least one; `brand`, `carrier`, `country`, `isFeatured`, `sort` and the picture belong to the service and are written to every language. A slug of the wrong form is `422` on `slug`, one in use `409 LISTING_EXISTS` on `slug`. A price under the floor and a locked or disabled service are refused as before.
- **The editor's Brand and Country fields (2026-11-17, owner request):** the wire is unchanged (`brand` is free text, `country` two letters or empty). The editor offers the brands the shop has a logo for (`lib/listingFacts.ts`, `SHOP_BRANDS`: the names of the website's `public/images/brands/*.png`, 65 files; add a name when a logo is added) with "Other" for a name typed by hand, and the 22 Arab countries; a country saved before that is not one of them stays selectable. Carrier is still free text.
- **Listing picture**: `POST /admin/service-listings/:id/image` (multipart, one part `file`; JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 2 MiB, 200 to 4096 px; answers the detail; `422` on `file` with `empty`, `unsupported_type`, `corrupt`, `animated`, `too_small`, `too_large_dimensions`, `rotated`; `413`; `415`) and `DELETE /admin/service-listings/:id/image` (`204`, also when there was none). The server's wording of the `file` message is shown as it comes. Both are routes beyond the map: the console's own manifest lists them as unknown tags (accepted, LEAD-DECISIONS section 2).
- **Categories** (`/admin/service-categories`, services feature): rows carry `nameAr` (`''` for none); `POST` and `PATCH` take `nameAr?` (at most 80, `''` clears).
- **Content pages** (`/admin/content-pages`): `kind` is `carrier`, `model`, `tool`, `brand`, `country` (landing) or `static`, `blog`; `status` is `draft`, `published` or `noindex`; rows carry `bodyMd` and, for landing pages, `visits30d` and `orders30d` (else `null`). The `kind` filter may repeat. Filters `q`, `kind`, `status`, `locale`; sorts `title`, `status`, `updatedAt`. `POST` body `{ kind, title, locale, slug?, bodyMd, seoTitle?, seoDescription? }` (created as a draft). `PATCH` takes `title`, `slug`, `locale`, `bodyMd`, `seoTitle`, `seoDescription`, never `kind` or `status`. `publish` body `{ published: boolean }`, answer the page. `generate` body `{ brands, models, networks }` (string lists), answer `{ created: ContentPage[], skipped: number }`.
- **Help articles** (`/admin/help-articles`): rows carry `answerMd`, `helpfulYes`, `helpfulNo`, `isPublished`, `sort`. Filters `q` (question, topic), `isPublished`; sorts `sort`, `question`, `category`, `isPublished`. `POST` body `{ category, question, answerMd, locale, isPublished? }`; the publish button is `PATCH { isPublished }`.
- **Legal documents** (`/admin/legal-documents`): `type` is `terms`, `privacy`, `refund` or `cookies`; rows carry `bodyMd`, `isCurrent`, `effectiveFrom` (`null` for a version not yet published), `requiresReacceptance` and `acceptedCount` (or `null`). The list answers every version (the console reads up to 200). `POST` body `{ type, locale, version, bodyMd, requiresReacceptance }` makes a version that is not in force; `publish` has no body and answers the published version.
- **TAC catalogue** (`/admin/tac-catalog`): filters `q` (prefix, brand, model, marketing name); sorts `tac`, `brand`, `model`, `releaseYear`. `POST` body `{ tac, brand, model, marketingName?, releaseYear? }`; `PATCH` any of them. `model` is the model code and `marketingName` the name customers know.
- **Language**: none (decision 039, 2026-11-16); no form offers one. See the note at the top.
- **Server messages**: a refusal names its field in `errors[]` (`retailPrice`, `serviceId`, `slug`, `version`, `tac`, `title`, `answerMd`…); anything else shows in the dialog's error slot or a toast.

## Feature 29 (Search tab)

The sixth tab, **Search** (`storefront:read`), tunes the shop's English search box (backend feature 29, plan `../backend/docs/briefs/29-search-typesense.md` §5.1 and §6; MISMATCHES M263). Four sections, kept in the address as `?tab=search&section=synonyms|pins|report|index` (Synonyms is the default and has no parameter). Files: `components/SearchTab.tsx` and `components/search/*`, `api/search.ts`, `hooks/useSearchAdmin.ts` (reads) and `hooks/useSearchMutations.ts` (writes), `types/search.ts`, `lib/search.ts` (pure helpers), `mocks/search{Routes,Seed,Report}.ts`.

- **Synonyms**: table (terms, kind, On switch, why, changed), Add and Edit dialog (terms as chips, kind multi or one-way with a root, a "Why" note), Delete behind a confirmation. Search `q`, status filter `state=on|off` (sent as `isEnabled`), sort `updatedAt`.
- **Pinned results**: "Test a search" box (`GET /admin/search/preview?q=`, saved rules only), table (query and match, pinned services by position, hidden count, Live / Scheduled / Ended / Off with the dates, On switch), Add and Edit dialog with a picker over `GET /admin/service-listings?locale=en&isListed=true&q=` (up to 10 pinned, 50 hidden, optional dates in the staff member's time zone). A refused service shows on its own row (`pinned.<i>.serviceId`, `hidden.<i>`); a twin query and match is `409 CONFLICT` under the words.
- **Report**: 7, 30 or 90 days; totals, top terms, searches that found nothing (row actions **Add synonym…**, **Pin services…**, **Hide from popular**), and the hidden terms with **Show again**. A term that looks like personal data (an `@`, a link, a leading `+`, more than five digits or a run of five) is never printed and has no actions; the server does not store those either.
- **Index**: engine state, documents against what the database expects, queued and failed updates, the last run of each job, the last error. **Rebuild index…** (`storefront:publish`) asks for confirmation; while `rebuild.running` the status is read every 5 seconds and the button waits.

Assumed contracts (the plan fixes every answer shape, not the bodies; `api/search.ts` is the one place to change): `POST` and `PATCH /admin/search/synonyms` take `{ kind, root?, terms, isEnabled?, note? }`; `POST` and `PATCH /admin/search/pins` take `{ query, match, pinned: [{ serviceId, position }], hidden: [serviceId], startsAt?, endsAt?, isEnabled?, note? }` (the answer carries titles); `POST /admin/search/hidden-terms` takes `{ term, note? }`; `GET /admin/search/report` takes `days` (7, 30 or 90); the three rule lists take `q`, `isEnabled`, `_sort` (`updatedAt`, and `query` for pins) and answer `x-total-count`; `POST /admin/search/index/rebuild` answers 202 with no body the console reads. Synonyms hold 2 to 20 terms of at most 60 characters whatever the kind (plan §4.6).
